10 AI Tricks Anyone Can Use Today

No tech skills needed. Open any free AI chat app (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot), copy a prompt below, swap in your own details, and press send.

💡 Tell it who you are and what you want🔁 Not quite right? Say "shorter", "friendlier" or "try again"🔒 Never paste passwords or full card numbers
Download the PDF
1✍️

Rewrite an email so it sounds just right

Say what you mean, kindly and clearly, in seconds instead of twenty minutes.

Copy & paste
Rewrite this email so it sounds friendly but firm. Keep it under 120 words and end with a clear next step:
[paste your email]
2📄

Explain a bill or letter in plain English

Insurance, medical and legal letters stop being scary when you know what they actually say.

Copy & paste
Explain this letter in plain English, like I'm not an expert. What is it asking me to do, by when, and is there anything I should question or double-check?
[paste the text, or attach a photo with account numbers covered]
3🥗

Plan meals from what's in your fridge

Less waste, fewer "what's for dinner?" moments, and no extra trip to the store.

Copy & paste
I have chicken thighs, rice, half a bag of spinach, eggs and a lemon. Give me 3 easy dinners for 2 people, under 30 minutes each, using mostly these. Add a short shopping list for anything missing.
4🎨

Make a flyer image

A good-looking flyer for your event, sale or business without hiring a designer.

Copy & paste
Make a bright, clean flyer for a neighborhood yard sale on Saturday, May 9, 8am to noon, at 123 Palm Street. Big readable text, a few fun sale items, lots of white space.

Tip: AI often misspells words inside images. Check the text, or add it yourself afterwards.

5📰

Summarize a long article

Get the main points of anything long in a few seconds, then decide if it's worth reading.

Copy & paste
Summarize this in 5 short bullet points, then tell me the one thing I should remember:
[paste the article or link]
6🗣️

Practice a hard conversation

Rehearse with zero pressure, so the real talk goes better.

Copy & paste
I need to ask my boss for a raise. Play my boss, who is busy and a bit skeptical. Let me practice one line at a time. When I say "done", give me 3 tips on what I could say better.
7✈️

Plan a trip

A full day-by-day plan built around how you like to travel, in one minute.

Copy & paste
Plan a relaxed 3-day trip to Savannah, Georgia for two people in their 60s. We love history and good food, and don't want too much walking. Give a day-by-day plan with rough costs.

Tip: double-check opening hours and prices before you book.

8🗂️

Organize a messy list

Turn a brain-dump into a clear plan you can actually start on.

Copy & paste
Here's my messy to-do list. Sort it into groups, put the most urgent things first, and break any big task into small steps:
[paste your list]
9🎓

Learn a new skill step by step

A patient personal tutor that goes at your pace and never gets tired of questions.

Copy & paste
I want to learn [Excel / ukulele / baking bread]. I'm a total beginner with 20 minutes a day. Give me a 2-week plan with one small lesson per day, and quiz me at the end of each week.
10🛡️

Stay safe: spot AI scams and deepfakes

Scammers can now fake voices, photos and videos. A few habits keep you and your family safe.

Copy & paste
I got this message. Does it show signs of a scam? List the red flags and tell me how to check if it's real without clicking anything:
[paste the message]

Also: agree on a family "safe word", and if a call feels urgent (gift cards, crypto, wire transfers), hang up and call back on a number you already know.
