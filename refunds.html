Refunds & cancellations

Fair and simple. Here's exactly what happens if plans change.

Last updated November 2026

Lessons (1-on-1, Zoom or in person)

  • Reschedule free with at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, or a no-show, is charged as a used lesson.
  • If we have to cancel, you choose a free reschedule or a full refund.
  • Packages (like the 3-lesson pack) are valid for 6 months from purchase.

Group classes

  • Full refund up to 48 hours before the class.
  • Within 48 hours, you get a credit toward a future class or lesson instead.
  • If we cancel a class, you get a full refund or a seat in the next one, your choice.

AI computer deposit ($99)

Your $99 deposit reserves your build and is fully refundable until parts are ordered. We'll confirm your exact price with you before ordering anything. Once you go ahead, the deposit is credited to your build.

Custom AI computers

  • All parts are new and carry their manufacturer's warranty.
  • We include 30 days of workmanship coverage on our assembly: if something we put together comes loose or wasn't connected right, we'll fix it at no cost.
  • Because every build is custom, returns of a completed build are limited to defects.
  • If a part arrives dead or fails early (DOA), it's handled through the manufacturer's warranty, and we'll help you every step of the way.

Setting up your existing PC ($149)

Same rules as lessons: reschedule free with 24 hours' notice; less than 24 hours or a no-show is charged.

Gift cards

  • Gift cards have no cash value and can't be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires.
  • Gift cards never expire.
  • Lost codes? Email us and we'll look it up.

How to ask

Email hello@example.com or text us. Refunds go back to your original payment method through Square, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
