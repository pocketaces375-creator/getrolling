Privacy policy

The short version: we collect only what we need to teach you and build your computer, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it any time.

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

GetRolling.ai is a trade name of Venderisgreat LLC, a Florida company based in Fort Lauderdale, FL, USA. "We" and "us" on this page means us.

What we collect

  • Your name and contact details (phone, email) when you message us, book, or ask for a price or our free guide.
  • Your messages, including what you tell us you want to do with AI.
  • Booking info: lesson times, what you booked, and notes that help us teach you.
  • Build details for AI computers: the parts you picked and your shipping address.
  • Payments are handled by Square. We see that you paid and the last few digits of your card, but never your full card number.

Cookies, Meta Pixel and analytics

Our site may use the Facebook/Meta Pixel and simple analytics cookies. They tell us things like which pages were visited and whether someone booked or asked for the guide, so we can see what works and show our ads to people likely to care. Meta may use this data under its own privacy policy.

You can block cookies in your browser settings, and you can control ad personalization in your Facebook/Meta account under ad preferences.

How we use it

  • To answer you, schedule and teach your lessons, and build and ship your computer.
  • To send you what you asked for (like the free guide) and the occasional useful tip. You can unsubscribe any time.
  • To keep basic business and tax records.

We don't sell your data

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services we need to run the business (like Square for payments, Zoom for lessons, email and calendar tools, and shipping carriers), or when the law requires it.

During lessons

We may see your screen while helping you. We won't record a lesson unless you agree first. Please don't share passwords with us; we'll show you how to type them in yourself.

Your choices, and how to ask for deletion

Want a copy of your info, a correction, or everything deleted? Email us at hello@example.com and we'll take care of it, usually within a few days. We may keep what the law requires (like payment records for taxes).

Kids

Our site is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Kids can take lessons with a parent or guardian who books for them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. This policy is governed by the laws of Florida, USA.

Questions? Email hello@example.com.
